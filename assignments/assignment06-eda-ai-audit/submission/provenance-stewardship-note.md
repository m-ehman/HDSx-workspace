# Provenance and Stewardship Note

This analysis uses the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom CSV snapshot (`nhanes_equity_v6.csv`). The snapshot was derived from public-use National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) files released by the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), covering 11 survey cycles between 1999-2000 and 2021-2023. The file is cached in the course repository at `examples/nhanes-equity/data/`, so class work needs no download or internet access. It supports descriptive classroom learning only, not population estimates, causal claims or individual-level inference, because the summaries do not account for the NHANES survey design. Row-level health records should not be uploaded to external AI tools, and any AI support should rely on aggregate summaries only.
